# Goals and the steward

The steward's ledger is the tutorial and the spine of a game. One card is shown at a time, each
with what to do, a hint naming the tab to use, and a reward. Completing a card unlocks buildings,
so the build bar grows as the village does. Locked cards still show their cost and say which goal
opens them, and newly unlocked buildings carry a "New" tag until you look at them.

In free build every building is open from the start and the cards give no rewards, but the steward
still tells the story.

## The chapters

| Chapter | What it teaches |
| --- | --- |
| The Hearth | Six people and one winter coming: wood, roofs, firewood. |
| The Fields | Farming and a year of food. |
| The Crafts | Planks, stone, tools, a manor. |
| The Realm | An heir, roads and trade, a militia, a second village. |
| The Rival Houses | Two houses on the same ledger; outrank them. |
| The Valley | Claims and campaigns; hold most of the valley. |

## Background cards

Some cards run in the background while the chain moves on, because they are about waiting or
about something that will happen on its own:

| Card | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| The valley stirs | any region changes hands for the first time |
| Survive the first winter | spring of year two with nobody buried |
| Raise a burgher house | one burgher house |
| Raise a townhouse | one townhouse |
| Bring a ram to the next gate | one battering ram in store |
| Turn back a campaign of theirs | one campaign of theirs repelled |
| Five regions under your banner | five regions |

They finish on their own terms with their own reward and chronicle line. The current card forgives
deaths at each new season; background cards do not.

## Rewards

Rewards are goods, gold, happiness or settlers: for instance 20 wood for the first hut, two
settlers for surviving the winter, 50 gold for standing above the rival houses. Every completed
card writes a line into the [Chronicle](dynasty-and-chronicle.md).

## After the ledger

The chain never stops play; it only gates buildings. When the last card is done the steward closes
his ledger and the game goes on as described in [Endgame](endgame.md). Reaching the last two
chapters opens the standing race against the rival houses.
