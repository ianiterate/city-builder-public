# Starting a game

## The start

Every game begins with six villagers aged 16 to 36 around a stockpile in a level clearing, holding
60 wood, 120 grain, 40 meat, 30 firewood, 12 tools and 12 clothing. The oldest adult is crowned lord.
Two rival houses are placed at least 26 tiles away. Only the stockpile and the woodcutter's hut are
open; the steward's cards open the rest (see [Goals and the steward](goals-and-the-steward.md)).
Free build opens everything at once.

## Difficulty

| | Gentle | Steady | Harsh |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starting goods | x1.75 | x1 | x0.6 |
| Raid odds | x0.5 | x1 | x2 |
| Event odds | x0.6 | x1 | x1.4 |
| Cold per day | x0.7 | x1 | x1.35 |
| Food eaten | x0.85 | x1 | x1.15 |
| Forest regrowth | x1.5 | x1 | x0.6 |

Difficulty is applied once at the start; a save replays exactly as it was played.

## Controls

- **Camera**: WASD or the arrow keys pan, Q and E rotate, the wheel or a pinch zooms toward the
  cursor, +/- and Page Up/Down zoom too, shift or alt with the wheel rotates, Home resets the view,
  O switches to a flat overhead view. Drag with the middle or right mouse button to pan.
- **Building**: click a card then the ground, or drag the card onto the map. The tool stays in
  hand; Escape, a right click or the card again puts it away. R is the road tool; roads and
  palisades are dragged as runs and shift-drag erases.
- **Screens**: G goals, V villagers, H the house, M the regional map, C the chronicle, L the log,
  F follows the selected villager, F11 fullscreen.
- **Time**: Space pauses; 1, 2 and 3 set the speed.
- **Saves**: F5 saves, F9 loads. In the browser use the menu instead of F5.

## Saves

There are two slots: your own quicksave (F5 or the menu) and the autosave, written at every turn of
the season, every three minutes of unpaused play, and when the browser tab is hidden.

In the browser the saves live in the site's storage, so a save belongs to the address you play at
and clearing site data removes it. "Download save" in the menu writes a file you can keep, and
"Load save from file" restores it anywhere, including a different browser or the Mac build. On the
Mac the saves are in the application support folder under CityBuilder/Saves.
