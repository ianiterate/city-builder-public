# Dynasty and chronicle

You are not a villager but a house. The house outlives its lords; the game ends only when the line
does.

## The lord

On day one the oldest adult is crowned lord of a house named Ashcombe, Harrowgate, Duncastle or
Wexley, and wed to the oldest free adult. Each lord draws two traits that colour the reign: how
much gold the taxes bring, how well the militia fights, and the like (see [Traits](traits-and-events.md)).

Villagers age four years per game year. A lord who is unmarried or widowed looks for a consort
every 30 days. Any child of the lord is an heir, oldest first. The lord, consort, heirs and a late
lord's widow carry the house name; the manor houses the ruling family.

## Succession

When the lord dies, the eldest living heir is crowned and the reign is written to the chronicle.
The village mourns for 30 days, up to 0.2 happiness fading over the month, twice that if the new
lord is a child. A weak succession, a child on the seat or a town below 0.4 happiness, has a 35 %
chance of drawing a rival claimant who walks out with 15 % of the common adults, the unhappiest
first.

If the lord dies with no heir, the house is finished. The world keeps turning and you can keep
watching, but nothing counts any more.

## Standing

Standing is the measure by which the houses of the valley rank each other, recomputed daily:

| Source | Standing |
| --- | --- |
| each villager | 1 |
| each housed adult | 0.5 |
| average happiness | 20 times it |
| each gold | 0.1 |
| each tile of held land | 0.05 |
| each point of the capital's defence | 0.5 |
| each civic building | 3 |
| each burgher household / townhouse | 2 / 4 |
| each generation of the house | 10 |
| each settlement | 15 |
| a wedding abroad, a house absorbed | a bonus |
| any trade pact standing | +5 % on the total |

Losing people, land, buildings or happiness lowers it. The rival houses are scored on the same
lines. Outranking both is a goal card, and staying above every house while holding most of the
valley is the [victory](endgame.md).

## The chronicle

Press C for the deeds of the house, year by year: regions gained and lost, campaigns, house
dealings, births, weddings and deaths, completed goals and the valley's turning points. The last
400 entries are kept and the list can be filtered by kind. The victory screen reads this same
ledger back as the story of your house.

## The house screen

Each rival house has a screen: who they are, what they hold, their army and standing broken down,
their attitude to you, what they are working on, and the five acts of diplomacy described in
[Regions and rivals](regions-and-rivals.md).
