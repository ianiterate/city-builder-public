# Endgame

## Victory

The race opens when the steward's ledger reaches its last chapters. From then the top bar counts
"dominant for N of 120 days". You are dominant on any day when both hold:

- you own at least three regions in five of the valley (rounded up), or the home region of every
  rival house;
- your standing is at or above every house still in the valley.

Hold both for 120 days in a row and you are **Lord of the Valley**. A valley with no houses left
in it, whether absorbed or driven out, is won outright. The victory screen shows the regions held,
the days counted, and reads the chronicle back as the deeds of your house. There is no score;
standing is the only number the houses are measured by.

## Defeat

The house is finished when:

- the **capital region**, the one the first settlement stands in, is lost to a house, or every
  region is; or
- the **lord dies with no heir** (see [Dynasty and chronicle](dynasty-and-chronicle.md)).

You may keep watching the valley turn, but nothing counts any more, or start a new game.

## The Age of the Valley

After victory the game continues. Nothing is switched off, the rival houses keep their armies and
their claims, and the world grows harder: raid odds and the houses' armies each rise by 10 % per
year since the victory, and the help given to houses that fall behind stops. Losing the capital
still ends the house. The victory screen names this age, and its only button is "Rule on".
