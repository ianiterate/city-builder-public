# City Builder — how the game works

A medieval city builder in the spirit of Banished and Manor Lords: individual villagers, seasons
that bite, a dynasty that outlives its lords, and two rival houses to outgrow or outfight for the
valley.

Play it in the browser: **https://ianiterate.github.io/city-builder-public/**

These pages explain the rules the game plays by, one system each, with the actual numbers. They
are kept in step with the game and republished with every build.

## In one paragraph

Six people arrive in a clearing with a winter coming. Cut wood, raise roofs, split firewood and
stock food, because hunger kills in five days and cold in four. Fields, workshops and a manor turn
the hamlet into a town with a lord, taxes and heirs. Two rival houses share the valley; they grow,
settle and covet on the same rules you play by. Regions fall to whoever presses the strongest
claim, by building, bordering, standing, marriage or war, and campaigns are settled off the map
with named dead. Hold three regions in five and outrank every house for 120 days to become Lord of
the Valley, then rule on into harder years.
